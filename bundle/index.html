Where the drop happens.

                
Read a YouTube Studio retention export and identify the moments worth reviewing. Analysis is based on the CSV's actual time axis.

              

            

            

              

                
                

                  
                  
                

                
Use the exported CSV from YouTube Studio. Percentage positions remain percentages unless a duration is supplied; the analyzer won't invent timestamps.

              

              

                
WHAT THE ANALYSIS CHECKS

                

                  
                  
Hook leak
Early retention loss in the first 30 seconds, or first 30% when the export uses a percentage axis.

                

                

                  
                  
Cliffs
Sudden drops between adjacent measurements. Every drop is shown with its actual position.

                

                

                  
                  
Slide
Average decline across the curve. A per-minute rate is only reported when elapsed time is known.
